# The Aravaan Trilogy — Manuscript Repository

> *"The world shifts beneath our feet, Prince Vyaan. The trials your generation shall inherit may demand virtues unknown to easier ages."*
> — Kaelvar, *From the Spire*

---

## About This Project

This repository is the working home for **The Aravaan Trilogy**, an epic political-fantasy manuscript of approximately 110,000 words across three linked volumes. The trilogy follows a young prince's return to a court corroded by secrecy, power, and the long shadow of a dead king's choices.

---

## Trilogy Structure

| Volume | Working Title | Timeline | Status |
|--------|--------------|----------|--------|
| **Book I** | *The Present Court* | Present day | In progress |
| **Codex** | *The Past King* | Historical — establishes origins | In progress |
| **Book II** | *Return to the Present* | Present day (continued) | Planned |

The **Codex** functions as a narrative hinge: it establishes the past king and his era in depth, and its revelations recontextualise everything the reader has seen in Book I when the story returns to the present in Book II.

## Key Characters

- **Vyaan** — Crown prince, freshly returned from the Iron Spire after eight years of austere training under the Sage Kaelvar
- **Asher** — Vyaan's assigned shadow; soldier of the Aionēkh Order, assigned by High Commander Eitan Uriel
- **Kaelvar** — The Sage of the Iron Spire; Vyaan's relentless teacher; trained Vyaan's father before him
- **Ohran** — Court figure; role and allegiance to be expanded in the cantos
- **Meera** — Court figure; thematic presence around loyalty and the cost of rule
- **Sanjela** — Court figure; linked to appearance/reality tensions in the capital
- **Nemyra** — Court figure; associated with old order vs. new order conflict

See [`manuscript/characters.md`](manuscript/characters.md) for full character profiles.

---

## Core Themes

- **Power and its corruption** — wealth, title, and court ritual masking institutional rot
- **Loyalty and legacy** — what is owed to a crown versus what is owed to conscience
- **New order vs. old order** — generational tension embodied in characters and symbols
- **Appearance and reality** — justice vs. seduction, the surface performance of court life
- **The cost of rule** — what training, sacrifice, and command actually require of a person

| Symbol | Resonance |
|--------|-----------|
| White roses | Purity, duty, and their inevitable staining |
| The circlet / crown | Legitimacy, burden, inheritance |
| Ritual | Institutional memory; the gap between ceremony and meaning |
| Perfume | Surface beauty; wealth masking decay |
| The Iron Spire | Discipline stripped of comfort; the formation of character under duress |
