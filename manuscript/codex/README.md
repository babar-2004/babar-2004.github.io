# The Codex

**Timeline:** The deep past  
**Subject:** The past king and the origins of the world's current order

The Codex is the hinge of the trilogy. It establishes why the present-day events of Book One and Book Two carry the weight they do — the decisions made long ago that now play out in the lives of Vyaan and those around him.
