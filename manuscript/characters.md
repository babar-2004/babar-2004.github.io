# Character Reference — The Aravaan Trilogy

This document is the master character reference for the trilogy. Entries are updated as cantos are completed. **Spoiler-level details are noted where relevant.**

---

## Primary Characters

### Vyaan
**Role:** Crown Prince of Aravaan; protagonist  
**First appearance:** *From the Spire* (Book I, Chapter 1)

Vyaan returns to the capital after eight years under the iron discipline of Kaelvar at the Iron Spire. He is intelligent, sharp-tongued when at ease, and acutely aware that the training was meant to forge him into something his father was not. His banter masks genuine anxiety about what he is returning to.

**Character arc seeds:**
- His father's legacy as a cautionary template (Kaelvar's "I believed a crown could ripen a boy into a man")
- The gap between the prince he was trained to be and the court he is returning to
- Whether the virtues of the Spire are adequate currency in a world of court politics

**Voice:** Conversational and slightly self-deprecating when relaxed; clipped and authoritative when threatened; introspective in moments of transition

---

### Asher
**Role:** Vyaan's shadow/bodyguard; soldier of the Aionēkh Order  
**Assigned by:** High Commander Eitan Uriel  
**First appearance:** *From the Spire* (Book I, Chapter 1)

Asher carries himself with the economy of someone who has been a weapon for a long time and forgotten how to be anything else. His hypervigilance is absolute — he tracks birds, squirrels, shadows. He shares almost nothing about himself, though his Aionēkh tattoo (compass rose, radiant sun, ocean waves) marks his allegiance unmistakably.

**Character arc seeds:**
- The meaning (or irony) of his name: "blessed and happy"
- What he knows about the danger on the roads that he cannot or will not say
- Whether his stoicism ever cracks — and what would cause it

---

### Kaelvar
**Role:** The Sage of the Iron Spire; Vyaan's teacher  
**First appearance:** *From the Spire* (Book I, Chapter 1)

A figure of deliberate granite stillness. Kaelvar does not teach; he forges. He stripped Vyaan of every comfort and illusion over eight years. His revelation that he trained Vyaan's father differently — and that he regrets the indulgence of that approach — is the first indication that this is a story shadowed by a previous generation's failure.

**Character arc seeds:**
- His evolution between training Vyaan's father and training Vyaan
- "I was... different then. Less thorough in my methods."
- What he knows about the coming danger that made him train Vyaan so relentlessly
