# Worldbuilding Reference — The Aravaan Trilogy

This document tracks the world's geography, institutions, cultural systems, and key terminology as established across the cantos.

---

## The World

### Aravaan / Amravan
The kingdom in which the present-day narrative is set. The capital city contains **seven domes** that rise above the ancient **Reivi River** and are visible from a great distance. The city sits beyond the **Barānmōr Dam**, with the mountain range behind it still visible from the valley approach.

> *"Eight years he had carried this image in his mind, and now it bloomed before him in full — the seven magnificent domes of the capital, rising like golden crowns across the ancient Reivi River."*

**Spelling note:** *Aravaan* is the kingdom's name; *Amravan* appears to be the capital city (or an alternate/poetic name for the same). Consistency across cantos should be confirmed.

---

### The Iron Spire
A training institution in the mountain heights, administered by the Sage Kaelvar. Described as perpetually shadowed, its grounds enclosed by iron gates. Students arrive as young as thirteen and may remain for years. The training is total: physical, strategic, psychological. Comforts are systematically stripped. The Spire is where the royal family sends its heirs to be made fit to rule.

---

### The Crown's Abode
The seat of royal power in the capital. Referenced by Asher when urging Vyaan's return. Possibly the palace complex.

---

## Institutions

### The Aionēkh Order
A military/protective order operating under a High Commander. Members carry a distinctive tattoo: a **compass rose crowned by a radiant sun, with ocean waves curling along the outer ring**, burned into the left forearm. Members are described as having military precision and hypervigilant training. The Order operates in the capital's security infrastructure and may have intelligence/investigative functions (implied by their awareness of dangers on the roads that they cannot publicly disclose).

**High Commander:** Eitan Uriel  
**Known members:** Asher

## Cosmology / Religion (emerging)

- References to "gods" in the plural suggest a polytheistic world
- The Aionēkh Order's imagery (sun, compass, waves) may have religious or cosmological resonance
- Ritual appears to carry institutional and possibly spiritual significance (developed further in the court cantos)

## Timeline

The trilogy spans two time periods:

| Layer | Period | Volume |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Present day | Vyaan's return and the court crisis | Book I, Book II |
| Historical | The past king's era | Codex |

The Codex is designed to be read between Book I and Book II, with its revelations reshaping the reader's understanding of everything in Book I retroactively.
